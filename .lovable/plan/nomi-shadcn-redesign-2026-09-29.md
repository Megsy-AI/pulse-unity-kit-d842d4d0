# Nomi shadcn redesign

## Goal
Rebuild Nomi's visual presentation so it feels polished and distinctive while preserving the existing character, flows, data, and features.

## Design direction
- Use shadcn components as the interface foundation, including buttons, sheets, cards, inputs, switches, badges, and separators.
- Apply the selected Paper & Cobalt palette: warm paper background, near-black text, quiet gray dividers, and focused cobalt actions.
- Use Space Grotesk for headings and DM Sans for body text.
- Keep a centered conversation layout with no bottom navigation.
- Favor crisp editorial spacing, modest corner radii, thin separators, and restrained shadows over pills, gradients, or glass effects.

## Screens
- Refine the first-time welcome screen with stronger scale, reduced empty space, and a clearer path into registration.
- Restyle registration and onboarding into a cohesive character-customization flow.
- Recompose chat around the character seated above the shadcn prompt input, clean suggestions, readable messages, and a borderless thinking state.
- Refine the top header and shadcn side sheet, with page links at the top and the user/settings entry at the bottom.
- Carry the same visual system through projects, tasks, memory, calls, character settings, integrations, privacy, and general settings.

## Validation
- Check the central first-time flow and chat on mobile and desktop.
- Verify English and Arabic layouts, light and dark themes, navigation, character customization, and contextual integration prompts.
- Confirm the app builds cleanly without changing backend behavior.
